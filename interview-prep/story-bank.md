# Story Bank — Master STAR+R Stories

This file accumulates your best interview stories over time. Each evaluation (Block F) adds new stories here. Instead of memorizing 100 answers, maintain 5-10 deep stories that you can bend to answer almost any behavioral question.

## How it works

1. Every time `/career-ops oferta` generates Block F (Interview Plan), new STAR+R stories get appended here
2. Before your next interview, review this file — your stories are already organized by theme
3. The "Big Three" questions can be answered with stories from this bank:
   - "Tell me about yourself" → combine 2-3 stories into a narrative
   - "Tell me about your most impactful project" → pick your highest-impact story
   - "Tell me about a conflict you resolved" → find a story with a Reflection

### [Systems Visibility] Catholic Health Telemetry Dashboard
**Source:** Report #002 — KBR — Junior Systems Engineer
**S (Situation):** Engineers needed better visibility across a large hospital-server environment and were losing time gathering context during operational reviews.
**T (Task):** Help build a clearer operational view and make it easier for engineers to spot unhealthy systems quickly.
**A (Action):** Built Spring Boot services and a React monitoring dashboard that aggregated telemetry from 500+ servers into one view, and supported secure alert-context flows.
**R (Result):** Engineers identified unhealthy servers about 25 percent faster, and alert context gathering became about 5x faster.
**Reflection:** Systems work often creates the most value when it improves signal quality and operator clarity rather than adding complexity.
**Best for questions about:** monitoring, systems support, operational tooling, debugging, engineering impact

### [Workflow Automation] A and L Reporting Automation
**Source:** Report #002 — KBR — Junior Systems Engineer
**S (Situation):** Monthly inventory reporting depended on slow spreadsheet-based prep spread across multiple workflows.
**T (Task):** Build a more reliable and repeatable reporting process for business users.
**A (Action):** Wrote Python ETL scripts and helped build a Flask/PostgreSQL system that consolidated 5+ workflows into one operational tool.
**R (Result):** Reporting preparation time dropped by 95 percent.
**Reflection:** Data and automation work becomes more powerful when it removes recurring work for the people closest to the process.
**Best for questions about:** data analysis, automation, process improvement, business impact

### [Cross-Functional Delivery] A and L Internal APIs
**Source:** Report #002 — KBR — Junior Systems Engineer
**S (Situation):** Operations and finance teams depended on ad hoc requests and spreadsheet lookups to answer routine inventory questions.
**T (Task):** Make core inventory and margin data easier to access and reduce interruptions for the team.
**A (Action):** Built REST APIs for inventory, lead times, and margins, and worked directly with stakeholders on feature needs and reliability.
**R (Result):** Ops-team email requests dropped by roughly 30 percent.
**Reflection:** Cross-functional engineering is strongest when the system changes how other teams work, not just how the code looks.
**Best for questions about:** stakeholder communication, API design, internal tools, teamwork
