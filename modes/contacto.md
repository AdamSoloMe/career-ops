# Mode: contacto - LinkedIn Power Move

1. **Identify targets** via WebSearch:
   - Hiring manager for the team
   - Assigned recruiter
   - 2-3 peers on the team with similar roles
   - Interviewer, if the candidate already has an interview scheduled

2. **Classify contact type** - ask the candidate or infer from context:
   - **Recruiter** - talent acquisition, sourcing, or recruiting role
   - **Hiring Manager** - the person leading the team that is hiring
   - **Peer** - someone in a similar role on the team who could become an indirect referral
   - **Interviewer** - someone scheduled to interview the candidate

3. **Choose the primary target**: the person who would benefit most from having the candidate on the team

4. **Generate the message** using a 3-sentence framework adapted to the contact type:

   ### Recruiter
   - **Sentence 1 (Fit):** Direct fit criteria - role, relevant experience, availability, or location
   - **Sentence 2 (Proof):** A detail that answers screening questions before they ask them
   - **Sentence 3 (CTA):** "Happy to share my CV if this aligns with what you're looking for"

   ### Hiring Manager
   - **Sentence 1 (Hook):** A specific challenge their team is facing, pulled from the JD, company blog, or news
   - **Sentence 2 (Proof):** The candidate's strongest quantified achievement showing they have solved similar problems
   - **Sentence 3 (CTA):** "Would love to hear how your team is approaching [specific challenge]"

   ### Peer (referral)
   - **Sentence 1 (Interest):** A genuine reference to their work - blog post, talk, open source project, or post
   - **Sentence 2 (Connection):** Something the candidate is working on in the same space, without making it a job pitch
   - **Sentence 3 (CTA):** "I've been working on similar problems at [company], would love to hear your take on [topic]"
   - **Note:** Do not ask for a job directly. The referral should happen naturally if the conversation develops.

   ### Interviewer (pre-interview)
   - **Sentence 1 (Research):** Reference something specific from their work or background
   - **Sentence 2 (Context):** A light connection to the candidate's experience in that area
   - **Sentence 3 (CTA):** "Looking forward to our conversation on [date]"
   - **Note:** Keep the tone light, not needy. The goal is to show preparation.

5. **Versions:**
   - EN by default
   - ES if the company is Spanish-speaking

6. **Alternative targets** with a short rationale for why they are strong second choices

**Message rules:**
- Maximum 300 characters
- No corporate-speak
- No "I'm passionate about..."
- Include something that makes them want to reply
- Never share a phone number
- The contact type changes the emphasis, not the structure
